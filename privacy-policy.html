Serponix — Privacy Policy

Game: Serponix: Live Snake Arena IO (package com.etherframe.serponix)
Developer: EtherFrame
Effective date: 1 November 2026

Overview

Serponix is a casual snake arena game. It does not require an account or sign-up, and it is designed to collect as little information as possible. This policy explains the limited data involved when you play.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The game has no registration, no login, and no analytics or advertising SDKs in this version.

  • Player name: You type a display name to play. It is stored only on your own device (in local storage) so you do not have to type it again. We do not receive or store it on any database.
  • Online play: When your device is online, the game connects to the Serponix game server so players can share one arena. During a session the server relays live gameplay data between players — your chosen display name, your snake's position and size, and game events. This data exists only in memory while the session runs, is not written to a database, and is not used for profiling or marketing.
  • Offline play: With no connection, the game runs entirely on your device against computer-controlled snakes and sends nothing anywhere.

How information is used

Gameplay data is used only to run the live arena while you play. We do not sell, rent, or share data with third parties, and we do not use data for advertising.

Children

Serponix is suitable for everyone. Because the game collects no personal information, no special children's data handling applies. Parents should note that the display name a player types is visible to other players in the same arena during online play, so children should not use their real name.

Data retention and security

Session gameplay data disappears when the session ends. Your display name and best score remain only on your device and can be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Serponix adds features that change what data is involved (for example advertising or voice chat), this policy will be updated first and the new version will be posted at this same address.
